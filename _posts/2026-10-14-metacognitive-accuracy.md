# Lab Note 001: Metacognitive Accuracy

*Does AI change how accurately we judge our own performance?*

## The Question

When people use artificial intelligence, does it change
how accurately they evaluate their own performance?

This question relates to metacognition: our ability to
monitor and evaluate our own thinking.

## The Concept

Metacognitive accuracy can be measured by comparing
a person's estimated performance with their actual
performance.

A smaller estimation error indicates greater accuracy.

## The Demonstration

I built a simple interactive tool that compares
estimation errors before and after a hypothetical
interaction with AI.

The tool calculates absolute estimation error and
identifies whether accuracy improved, deteriorated,
or remained unchanged.

[Try the interactive demonstration]({{ '/experiments/metacognitive-accuracy/' | relative_url }})

## Limitations

This is a computational demonstration, not a
completed scientific experiment.

The tool uses manually entered scores and does not
verify AI usage, collect participant data, or establish
causal effects.
